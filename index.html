IT Support Automation Toolkit

Complete

PowerShell scripts that turn the most repetitive help desk tasks into one command, with audit logging and a preview mode on every change. Built and tested in my Active Directory home lab.

What it does
  • Onboards new hires: AD account, naming convention, OU, department groups and home folder, one at a time or in bulk from CSV
  • Offboards securely: disables (never deletes), scrambles the password, archives group memberships, records the ticket number
  • Resets passwords in bulk and unlocks locked accounts after a mass lockout
  • Generates a color-coded HTML health report for slow-PC triage across many machines
